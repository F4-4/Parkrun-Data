Parkrun Data

Parkrun is a global movement of free, weekly, community-led 5km events held in parks and public open spaces every Saturday morning. Founded in 2004 by Paul Sinton-Hewitt at Bushy Park in London, England, it began as a simple gathering of 13 runners and a few volunteers. Today, parkrun has expanded into an international family across over 20 countries, bringing together millions of runners, joggers, walkers, and volunteers of all abilities in a welcoming, non-competitive environment.
This dashboard provides a comprehensive visual analysis of global and UK parkrun engagement, tracking weekly participation trends, event counts, volunteer sign-ups, and runner milestones across the worldwide parkrun community.
